Unit II Syllabus Topics

Relational Database Model, Logical View of Data, Keys in Relational Database, Integrity Rules, Relational Algebra, Selection, Projection, Join, Division, Relational Calculus, Tuple Relational Calculus, Domain Relational Calculus, Normalization, First Normal Form (1NF), Second Normal Form (2NF), Third Normal Form (3NF), Boyce-Codd Normal Form (BCNF), SQL, Aggregate Functions, GROUP BY and HAVING Clauses, Null Values, Inner Join, Left Join, Right Join, Full Outer Join, Nested Subqueries, Joined Relations.
